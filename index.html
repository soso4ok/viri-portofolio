Truth Tails

An educational puzzle game about misinformation, set in Felinsburg—a city of animals where the social network "Meowitter" shapes reality.

Concept &
Premise

Why misinformation? Why cats? The jam theme was Ctrl+Alt+Disinfo—a prompt that pushed us toward the social media space. We wanted the setting to feel familiar yet slightly absurd, so players notice parallels with real-world platforms without being preached to.

A satirical anthropomorphic city with its own Twitter clone, "Meowitter", gave us that distance. The protagonist, Kio, is relatable because his situation is painfully ordinary: caught in a viral lie, powerless—until he decides to fight back.

Design
Systems

My focus was defining the core loop early: the fundamental action the player repeats. We landed on:

Receive → Analyse → Judge

This loop mirrors real media literacy habits, making the educational layer feel earned rather than imposed.

  • Fact-checking
    Players cross-reference posts against clues, building the habit of source verification through gameplay.
  • Trust Meter
    A reputation system that rewards careful decisions and punishes snap judgments.
  • UI as World
    The game's UI is the game world. I designed the Meowitter interface to feel immediately legible, yet increasingly manipulative as the story escalates.

The Outcome

Built over three months by a team of five. For me, it was the first project where I took full ownership of game mechanics design, UI systems, and Unity implementation simultaneously—delivering a ranked, grant-winning result.
